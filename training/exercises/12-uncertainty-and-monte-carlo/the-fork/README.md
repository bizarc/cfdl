# Un-blend the rollover

The starter averages a 70% renewal and a 30% vacancy into 81,700 a month — a rent that no future actually pays.

1. Replace the blend with the fork: a uniform draw, and a branch that commits each trial to one world.
2. Run.
3. Put the deterministic result and the Monte Carlo percentiles side by side.

What to find: the deterministic case draws its central 0.5 and renews. The trials split — about seven hundred renewal paths, and about three hundred with a dark quarter and a discounted re-let. Locate the blended starter's answer relative to the two clusters. The answer sits *between* worlds, which is precisely the problem with blends: a reserve sized on the average is wrong in every path that occurs.
