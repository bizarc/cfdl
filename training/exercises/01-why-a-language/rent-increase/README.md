# A rent increase, as claims

The workshop's landlord raises the rent 5% for the second year.

1. Split the single rent stream into two streams.
2. Set year one to 4,200 a month.
3. Set year two to the expression `4200 * 1.05`, not a pre-computed number. The model must show the base and the increase, the way you would state the terms aloud.

Before you run, compute the expected change: the lifetime rent total rises by 4,200 × 0.05 × 12 = 2,520 over the starter's.
