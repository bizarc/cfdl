# Widen the claims, split the proceeds

Two final moves.

1. Turn the deal's three contested numbers into distributed assumptions, deferred into the terms: office rent (Normal), the monthly draw (Triangular), the exit cap (Normal).
2. Add a downside scenario and a thousand seeded trials to the run configuration.
3. Add the equity split as a waterfall at exit. Pay the LP's preferred and capital, then the GP's 20% promote. Draw the pot from the sale proceeds, net of the loan payoff.
4. Run. Read the scenario table and the trial statistics against it. Confirm the downside NPV goes negative.

Note what happens to the base case. The deterministic draw for a Triangular is the mean of its three points: 720,000, not the 700,000 mode. Stating the width *moved the base case*, and the construction budget got 340,000 heavier. Not a bug: that is what asymmetric risk means.
