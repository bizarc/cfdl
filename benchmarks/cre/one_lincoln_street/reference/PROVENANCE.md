## What was read

**Exhibit 7 — Construction Period Interest Schedule.** Sixteen quarters,
2000.1 through 2003.4. Columns transcribed: Required Funding, Equity Funding,
Debt Funding, Interest Expense, and the opening and closing debt balances. Also
the three stated drivers above the table: net construction period equity
funding $110,738,000, an annual construction interest rate of 8.00% compounded
quarterly, and the note that funding is "assumed to occur ratably throughout
the quarter".

**Exhibit 6 — Construction Period Funding Schedule** for the same sixteen
quarters, whose totals agree with Exhibit 7's Required Funding column
($285,145,000).

## Nothing here is fitted

The model states only the draw schedule, the rate and the equity commitment.
The equity/debt split, the opening balances and the interest are all computed
from those three, and are compared against the exhibit rather than read from
it. The mid-quarter crossover in 2001.4 — where the equity commitment runs out
and the quarter splits 10,522,000 / 18,908,000 — falls out of the arithmetic
rather than being stated.

**Exhibit 5, the eleven-year operating pro forma, is deliberately not used.**
Its derived lines reconcile internally (2004: EGI 56,059, NOI 37,257, before-tax
cash flow 37,100, each recomputed from its components), but the lease-level
assumptions that drive its rent, absorption and reimbursement lines are not
published. Asserting it would mean feeding in most of the answer.

The PDF is committed as data and is never opened or executed by any test; the
figures were read once and transcribed.
