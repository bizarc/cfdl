# Licensing

CFDL is **source available** under the
**Business Source License 1.1**
(SPDX: `BUSL-1.1`). It is not an open-source license, and we do not describe
CFDL as open source.

## What you can do

- Read, copy, and modify the source.
- Make non-production use of any kind (development, evaluation, testing,
  research, education).
- Make **production use** — run CFDL inside your organization, in your own
  models, pipelines, and products.

## What requires a commercial license

Offering CFDL itself to third parties on a hosted or embedded basis, in a
product or service, in exchange for a fee or other commercial benefit. If you
want to sell CFDL-as-a-service or ship it inside a commercial product,
contact the maintainers for a commercial license.

## Change license

Each released version converts to the **Apache License, Version 2.0** on its
Change Date — four years after that version's release. The `LICENSE` file distributed with each
release carries the exact parameters and is authoritative if anything on this
page disagrees with it.
